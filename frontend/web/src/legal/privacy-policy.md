# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 28, 2026**

Yadhum ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by Yadhum Team, Albany, New York, USA. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Yadhum web application and Android app (package: net.yadhum.app), how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By creating an account or using Yadhum, you agree to this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information you provide directly

When you create an account, we collect:

- **Name** – your first and last name, as you enter them during signup
- **Email address** – used to identify your account and send transactional messages (e.g., password reset)

We do not collect your phone number, date of birth, address, or payment information. Yadhum is free and has no in-app purchases.

### Information collected automatically

When you use Yadhum, we automatically receive:

- **Usage data via Google Analytics** – page views, session duration, general location (country/region), device type, and browser. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you. Analytics are collected via Google Analytics (measurement ID: G-BBETP884QB).
- **Performance and crash data** – if you use the Android app, Google Play's built-in Android Vitals may collect crash reports and ANR (App Not Responding) data. We do not use any additional third-party crash-reporting SDK.
- **Authentication tokens** – a session token is stored in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Create and manage your Yadhum account
- Personalise your learning experience (e.g., track which lessons you have completed)
- Send password-reset or other account-related emails
- Understand how students use the app so we can improve it (via aggregated analytics)
- Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use your personal data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

## 4. Cookies and Local Storage

Yadhum does not use tracking cookies. We use:

- **Browser local storage** – to store your authentication token so you stay signed in between sessions. This data stays on your device.
- **Google Analytics cookies** – set by Google to measure aggregate usage. You can opt out via your browser settings or a browser extension such as the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Yadhum is designed for Samacheer Kalvi Plus 1 and Plus 2 students (typically ages 16–18). The service is **not directed at children under the age of 13**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it promptly. If you believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at yadhumedu@gmail.com.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your account data (name and email) for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

---

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate data
- **Delete** your account and associated personal data
- **Object** to or **restrict** certain processing

To exercise any of these rights, please email **yadhumedu@gmail.com** with the subject line "Privacy Request" and a description of your request. We will respond within 30 days.

---

## 8. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Yadhum account at any time. Your account and all associated personal data (name, email, learning progress) will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request.

**To delete your account:**

1. Visit [yadhum.net/delete-account](https://yadhum.net/delete-account)
2. Follow the instructions on that page, or email **yadhumedu@gmail.com** with the subject "Delete My Account"

## 9. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), Supabase row-level security, and hashed credential storage. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
