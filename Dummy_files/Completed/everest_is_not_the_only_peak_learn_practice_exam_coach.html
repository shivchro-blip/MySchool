About the Poet

Prof. V. C. Kulandaiswamy, known as Kulothungan, lived from 1929 to 2016. The textbook introduces him as an eminent technologist, a man of letters and an educationist known for his pioneering contributions in Hydrology, Literature and Education.

He authored six volumes of poems and seven volumes of prose essays. His literary work earned him the Thiruvalluvar Award from the Government of Tamil Nadu in 1999. He also won the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1988 for his book Vaazhum Valluvam. His works deal with themes that reflect the whole range of human progress and all-pervasive human effort.

Why this matters for this poem: “Everest is not the Only Peak” is not just about climbing mountains. It is a poem about human dignity, duty, devotion and self-respect. Knowing the poet as a technologist, educationist and man of letters helps us understand why he values effort, competence, merit and honour in every field of life.

Before We Read

The warm-up asks students to identify great personalities, their fields of achievement, remarkable achievements and admirable qualities. This prepares us for the poem’s central idea: greatness is not limited to one famous height or one famous achiever. Every field has its own peak, and every person can rise with devotion and dignity.

Everest is not the only peak, Every hillock has a summit to boast!

The poem tells us that even a small hill has its own summit. In life, a person need not become the greatest in the world to be honourable. One must do one’s duty with courage, devotion and self-respect.
