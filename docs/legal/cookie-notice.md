# Cookie Notice

**Last updated:** 2026-05-11
**Service:** TN Exam Coach

---

## What we store

TN Exam Coach does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We store the following in your browser's local storage:

| Key | Purpose | Essential? |
|-----|---------|-----------|
| `exam_coach_token` | Keeps you logged in between sessions | Yes — disabling this logs you out |
| `tnec_theme` | Remembers your light/dark mode preference | No |
| `tnec_cookie_consent` | Records your cookie banner choice | No |

On mobile, we use secure device storage (not cookies) for the session token and app preferences.

## Analytics

We use basic, self-hosted analytics to understand which lessons students find most useful. This data is aggregated and not linked to advertising networks.

If you choose "Continue with essential cookies only", analytics collection is disabled.

## How to clear

You can clear all stored data by logging out and clearing your browser's local storage, or by uninstalling the mobile app.
