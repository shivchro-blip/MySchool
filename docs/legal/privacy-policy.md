## 1. Who we are

TN Exam Coach is a free educational website and mobile app operated by Sam ("we", "our", or "us"), an individual based in Tamil Nadu, India. It is not a registered company.

Contact: privacy@tnexamcoach.in

## 2. What data we collect

We collect the following data when you create an account:

- **Email address** — used to identify your account and send essential service notices.
- **Password** — stored as a one-way hash (never in plain text) by our auth provider (Supabase).
- **Age confirmation** — whether you are 18+ or under 18 with parental consent. Stored as a text label.
- **Consent timestamps** — the date and time you agreed to these terms and the Privacy Policy.

We collect the following data while you use the service:

- **Your answers to practice questions** — stored to show you your attempt history.
- **AI evaluations of your answers** — scores and feedback generated by our AI model.
- **Usage logs** — which features you use and when, used for rate limiting and service improvement.

We do **not** collect: name, phone number, school name, payment details, or location.

## 3. How we use your data

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Email | Account identification, essential service emails |
| Answers & evaluations | Show your attempt history and progress |
| Usage logs | Rate limiting, understanding which lessons are useful |
| Consent timestamps | Legal compliance record |

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

## 4. Who we share data with

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

| Service | Purpose | Data shared |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Supabase | Database and authentication | Email, hashed password, answers, usage logs |
| Ollama (local) | AI evaluation (self-hosted) | Practice answer text |
| OpenRouter (fallback) | AI evaluation fallback (paid users only) | Practice answer text |

## 5. Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage (not advertising cookies) for:

- **Session token** (`exam_coach_token`) — keeps you logged in. Essential; cannot be disabled.
- **Theme preference** (`tnec_theme`) — remembers your light/dark mode choice.
- **Cookie consent choice** (`tnec_cookie_consent`) — remembers your banner choice.

On mobile, we use secure device storage for the session token and app preferences.

## 6. Data retention

We retain your account data as long as your account exists. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 8).

Usage logs older than 90 days are automatically deleted.

## 7. Children and minors

TN Exam Coach is designed for students, including minors. Students under 18 must confirm that a parent or guardian has agreed to their use of the service. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent.

## 8. Your rights

You may, at any time:

- **Access** the data we hold about you — email us.
- **Correct** inaccurate data — email us.
- **Delete** your account and data — email us with subject "Data deletion request". We will respond within 30 days.
- **Withdraw consent** — you may stop using the service at any time.

## 9. Security

Passwords are stored using bcrypt hashing by Supabase Auth. All data is transmitted over HTTPS.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We will update the "Last updated" date at the top when we make material changes. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
